## What data we collect

### Data you provide directly
- Full name
- Annual salary
- Monthly savings amount
- Retirement contribution percentage
- Existing portfolio value
- Investment time horizon
- Immigration status
- Risk tolerance
- Investment goal
- Whether you have an emergency fund

This information is collected once during onboarding and stored in your profile. You are never asked for it again.

### Data collected through Plaid
If you choose to connect a bank or brokerage account, we access the following through Plaid, a third-party financial data provider:

- Account names and types (checking, savings, investment, retirement)
- Account balances
- Institution names

We do not access transaction history, account numbers, routing numbers, or any other data beyond what is required to show you your current financial position.

Connecting a bank account is optional. The app functions without it, using your manually entered profile data instead.

### Authentication data
We use Clerk for authentication. When you create an account, Clerk collects your email address and manages your credentials. We do not store your password. Optionally, you may sign in with Google SSO — in that case Google's privacy policy also applies.

### Investment decision data
Every investment recommendation generated by the app is logged, including the date and time, market context seen at the time of the recommendation, the allocations recommended, and trade receipts from the brokerage. This log is your audit trail.

## How we use your data

We use your data for one purpose: to generate personalized daily investment recommendations.

Specifically:
- Your financial profile is passed to an AI model (currently Claude by Anthropic) to generate investment allocations suited to your goals, risk tolerance, and financial situation
- Your connected account balances are pulled at the time you request a recommendation to give the AI model accurate context about your current financial position
- Your investment history is stored so you can review past decisions

We do not use your data for advertising. We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with third parties except as described below.

---

## Who we share data with

| Party | Purpose | Data shared |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| Anthropic (Claude API) | Generate investment recommendations | Your financial profile, market context, account balances |
| Plaid | Connect bank accounts | Your authorization only — Plaid retrieves account data directly from your institution |
| Alpaca | Execute paper trades | Ticker symbols and dollar amounts only — no personal or account data |
| Clerk | Authentication | Email address, session tokens |
| MongoDB Atlas | Database hosting | All stored user data, encrypted at rest |

No party listed above is permitted to use your data for their own purposes beyond providing the service to InvestIQ.

---

## How we protect your data

- All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or higher)
- Plaid access tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM before being written to the database
- All secrets and API credentials are stored as environment variables, never hardcoded
- Every API endpoint is scoped to your user ID — no user can access another user's data
- Database access is restricted to the application server

---

## Plaid access tokens

When you connect a bank account through Plaid Link, Plaid issues an access token that allows InvestIQ to read your account data. This token is:

- Encrypted before being stored in our database
- Never shared with any third party other than Plaid itself
- Immediately revoked at Plaid and deleted from our database when you disconnect the account

You can disconnect any connected account at any time from your profile page. Disconnecting immediately and permanently revokes our access to that account.

---

## Autonomous investment (when enabled)

If you enable the auto-invest feature, InvestIQ will access your connected account balances once daily at approximately 9:00 AM to execute your daily investment. This access occurs without you being actively logged in.

By enabling auto-invest, you explicitly consent to this daily background access. Every background data access is logged with a timestamp and the data that was retrieved. You can disable auto-invest at any time, which immediately stops all background account access.

---

## Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active.

If you delete your account:
- Your financial profile is permanently deleted from our database
- All investment decision history is permanently deleted
- All Plaid access tokens are revoked at Plaid and deleted from our database
- Your authentication data is deleted from Clerk

We do not retain any personal data after account deletion.

## Your rights

You have the right to:
- **Access** — request a copy of all data we hold about you
- **Correction** — update your financial profile at any time from the profile page
- **Deletion** — delete your account and all associated data at any time
- **Disconnection** — disconnect any linked financial account at any time

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the CCPA including the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information), and the right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at krishnarajivvns@gmail.com.
